# v0.2.9

- **Save and apply to Steam** sets up Launch Options directly, with an account choice and a preview. Exit Steam normally before applying; the previous setting is backed up.
- **Restore previous Launch Options** brings back the recorded setting and preserves other games. Later manual changes are kept, and uncertain results retain recovery copies.
- Manual copying remains available. Older manually pasted commands can be cleared, but unknown previous arguments cannot be recovered.
