# Code of Conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

SteamWrapper welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and language abilities. This code applies to the project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific. Critique an idea or implementation without attacking its author.
- Accept good-faith corrections and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Give newcomers room to learn. English and Simplified Chinese participation are welcome.
- Respect consent, privacy, accessibility needs, and ownership of games and other work.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid repeated demands, unsolicited promotion, or disruptive posting.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized conduct, stalking, deliberate intimidation, and disclosure of someone else's private information are not acceptable. Do not retaliate against a person for raising a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and moderation

For harmful content on GitHub, use GitHub's reporting controls or [report abuse to GitHub](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). For a project moderation concern that can safely be discussed publicly, open an issue describing the behavior and linking the relevant public content. Do not publish private evidence or personal details. If a private conversation is necessary, request a private contact method without posting the sensitive material; the project currently lists no dedicated private conduct-reporting address.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close or lock a conversation, request a behavior change, or restrict participation using GitHub's moderation tools. Decisions should consider the conduct, context, and effect on other participants. Disagreement with a technical decision alone is not misconduct.

This repository does not list a dedicated moderation team or guaranteed response time. GitHub's own terms and community rules also apply. This is a project-specific code; it does not alter the software license.
